# Booked Job — Revival Strategy (data-backed, 2026-11-11)

**One line:** Booked Job is the **honest, data-backed reviews desk for service pros** — we spend the money and tell you the truth about the AI tools, software, and lead services sold to you, and we point you at the one that actually works (aaron.chat) or tell you to spend $0.

**Editorial law (non-negotiable):** Never shill AI. Every verdict is backed by real data — real pricing, real contractor complaints with real permalinks, real search/FTC/court facts. When the honest answer is "an AI receptionist is a $300/mo waste for a 2-truck shop," we say that. When the answer is "spend zero dollars," we say that. This honesty IS the moat — AI-slop review farms can't fake it.

## The three pillars (each mapped to what it's actually for)

**Pillar 1 — Tool & software reviews → SEO traffic + affiliate revenue.**
Field-service software (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Workiz) + AI receptionists (Smith.ai, Rosie, GoodCall) + the money comparisons (`X vs Y`, `best X`). Honest field reports via `review_render.py`. This is the **engine** — the searchable, monetizable core.

**Pillar 2 — Lead-service exposés → brand hook + aaron.chat leads.**
Angi, Thumbtack, HomeAdvisor, Bark, Networx. "Don't get ripped off." No affiliate — the Work Order CTA (aaron.chat) is the conversion. Highest emotional pull, real data (FTC, Trustpilot), feeds the reels.

**Pillar 3 — Marketing truth → the VOICE + social/video (not SEO).**
The blunt, data-backed "here's how you actually get work" takes. Delivered as the **device-fail reels** and short social, not standalone blog posts (no search demand). This is top-of-funnel discovery + authority.

## Website ↔ social harmonization (the core mechanic)

**Every review page has a companion reel; every reel lands on its review.**
- The **device-fail reel** ("AI receptionist loses the $9k job," "the $7 roof quote") = social discovery → drives awareness + comments.
- The **review page** (SEO capture for "is an ai receptionist worth it," "jobber vs housecall pro") = catches the searcher the reel created.
- Both end on the same **aaron.chat Work Order** (lead) + **affiliate ledger** (revenue).
- Reel caption/end → `booked-job.com/reviews/<slug>` (the Reviews Desk is the landing, not the generic homepage).

One topic → one review page + one reel + the syndication the automation already does. That's the harmonized unit.

## The lead engine (how the site actually converts)

Reviews = trust + traffic; aaron.chat = the lead; affiliate = passive revenue on the tools we genuinely endorse.
