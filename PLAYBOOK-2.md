# Booked Job — Playbook #2 (LOCKED 2026-08-17)

**Supersedes the original "post everywhere, build a following" play.** After ~6 weeks,
Playbook #1 produced 500+ pieces across 14 platforms → 11 FB followers, 1 IG follower,
0 YouTube subs, **7 email signups**. The one thing that pulled: **YouTube search views
(1,278)** on money/comparison topics. Lesson: **discovery/search beats broadcasting to
empty feeds, and the metric is signups — not followers.**

---

## THE NEW PLAY: "The Trades Marketing 101" — no-BS, start-from-zero

**The bet:** our #1 real-world problem — *service pros who don't even have a website* —
is also **Hey Aaron's core product** and a goldmine of **search-intent** topics. A guy
who Googles *"do I need a website for my plumbing business"* is raising his hand. We
answer him dead-simple, capture his email, and he becomes a **Hey Aaron web-design lead**
(or a Consent Resolve lead if he already has a site). **The education IS the funnel.**

This fixes both things the data proved broken:
- **Distribution** — these topics get found by *search*, the one surface that worked.
- **Conversion** — finally a reason to capture email and a place to send them (the $500
  website service / Consent Resolve).

## AUDIENCE & VOICE (hard guardrails)
- **ICP: the low-LOW-tech one-truck owner.** Never built a website. Doesn't know what a
  domain is. Thinks "SEO" is a scam word because someone cold-called him about it.
- **Keep the Booked Job voice** (sharp peer in the trade, money is the through-line, no
  hype) but dial simplicity **harder**:
  - ~5th-grade reading level. Short sentences.
  - **One idea per piece.**
  - **Define every jargon word in plain English the instant it appears** — e.g. *"SEO just
    means showing up when somebody Googles a plumber near them."*
  - Always tie back to money / booked jobs. No growth-hacks, no agency-speak, no hype.
  - Assume zero prior knowledge. Use examples from the trade (trucks, drywall, drains).

## FORMATS (updated per Aaron, 2026-08-17)

**1. Blog article** — the plain-English answer, one per topic. Compounding SEO asset.

**2. Short-form video** — one per topic, search-titled. **VISUALS MUST BE REWORKED:**
- *Problem:* current reels are white/yellow text on a dark "asphalt" charcoal background for
  the WHOLE video (make_reel.py). No imagery, no b-roll, no motion → reads as "black screen + VO."
- *Fix:* real visuals behind/around the text — relevant trade imagery (AI-generated via
  fal/Recraft, or stock), Ken Burns motion on stills, simple motion graphics for the money
  numbers, and screen-recordings for the "how to" pieces. Text stays as bold captions, but it
  is no longer the *only* thing on screen.

**3. Long-form video (NEW) — "Dark Docket" style.** Faceless, narrated, longer YouTube videos
(reuse the faceless-youtube-video skill + the Darkest Docket render infra). Good fit for the
curriculum: *"The Complete No-BS Marketing Guide for Contractors Who Are Starting From Zero,"*
or per-level deep dives. Long-form = watch-time + search authority on YouTube (the one channel
that pulled). Clip each long-form into the shorts.

**4. Lead magnet** — *"The No-BS Contractor Website & Marketing Starter Kit"* (checklist/PDF:
do you need a website, what it should cost, the 5 must-haves, your first $200 of marketing).
**Every piece of content points to it → email capture.**
