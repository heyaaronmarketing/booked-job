Workiz: Built for the Phone-Driven Trades

Workiz has one thing Jobber and Housecall Pro don't: a built-in phone system with call tracking. If your business lives and dies on inbound calls — locksmith, garage door, appliance repair, junk removal — that's a real edge. If you quote flat-rate HVAC or plumbing jobs, it's the wrong tool. Here's the honest split.

Verdict —<br>Great for call-heavy trades

Right tool, specific shop. Workiz's built-in VoIP, call tracking, and dispatch make it a genuinely strong pick for phone-driven, on-demand trades (locksmith, garage door, appliance repair, junk removal). But it's weaker on flat-rate pricing, estimating, and reporting, so HVAC, plumbing, and general contractors who need a price book are usually better on Jobber or Housecall Pro. Pricing is also less transparent — confirm it before you commit.

Finding 01

The one feature that sells it

Most field-service software makes you bolt on a phone system. Workiz builds one in — VoIP, call recording, and call tracking tied to your jobs. For a shop where every job starts with an inbound call and speed-to-answer wins the work, that's not a gimmick; it's the whole point. Reviewers consistently praise the mobile app, fast onboarding, and on-demand dispatch. It's rated solidly (Capterra ~4.4/5).

Questions, answered straight

Is Workiz worth it in 2026?
For phone-driven, on-demand trades — locksmiths, garage-door, appliance-repair, junk-removal — yes. Its built-in VoIP phone system and call tracking are a real advantage those shops won't get from Jobber or Housecall Pro. For HVAC, plumbing, or general contractors who need flat-rate pricing and strong reporting, it's usually the wrong fit.
How much does Workiz cost?
Sources disagree: there's a free/Lite tier for up to 2 users, with paid plans reported anywhere from ~$65 to ~$325/mo depending on the source and user count, plus a built-in phone add-on (~$19/user or ~$100/mo). Pricing transparency is weak — get a written quote before committing.
Workiz vs Jobber vs Housecall Pro?
Workiz wins on built-in phone/VoIP and on-demand dispatch for call-heavy trades. Jobber is cheaper and cleaner for most small shops. Housecall Pro offers the most breadth and financing. Pick by your trade: phone-driven → Workiz; flat-rate → Jobber or Housecall Pro.
