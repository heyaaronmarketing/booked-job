ServiceTitan: Powerful — and Not Built for You

ServiceTitan is the Ferrari of field-service software. It's genuinely powerful. It's also priced, contracted, and onboarded for franchise-scale operations — which means for a one-to-ten-truck shop it's usually the wrong, wildly expensive answer. Here's the honest math before a salesperson gets you on a 12-month contract.

Verdict —<br>Not for small shops

Only if you're big. ServiceTitan is a serious platform for large, multi-crew, dispatch-heavy operations — and it bills like it: reportedly $245–$400+ per technician per month, a five-figure implementation fee, and a 12-month contract. For most small shops that's tens of thousands a year to use a fraction of it. Run Jobber or Housecall Pro instead and revisit ServiceTitan when you're genuinely scaling.

Finding 01

It's good. That's not the question.

Let's be fair: ServiceTitan is a deep, capable platform — dispatch, call booking, marketing attribution, payroll, reporting, the works. Big HVAC, plumbing, and electrical operations run their whole business on it for a reason. The question was never “is it good software.” It's “is it the right software for your shop” — and for most of the people reading this, the price and the contract answer that for you.

Questions, answered straight

How much does ServiceTitan cost in 2026?
ServiceTitan doesn't publish prices, but contractors report roughly $245–$400+ per technician per month, a one-time implementation fee of about $5,000–$50,000, and a 12-month contract. For a 10-tech shop that's roughly $29,000–$48,000+ in year one for the subscription alone.
Is ServiceTitan worth it for a small contractor?
Usually not. It's built for large, multi-crew, franchise-scale operations, and the per-tech pricing plus five-figure setup is hard to earn back below that scale. Most small shops get the same core jobs done on Jobber or Housecall Pro for a fraction of the cost.
What should a small shop use instead of ServiceTitan?
Jobber for the cleanest, cheapest all-in-one, or Housecall Pro if you need built-in financing and multi-tech dispatch. Revisit ServiceTitan only when you're genuinely scaling into a large operation.
