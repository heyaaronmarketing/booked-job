HomeAdvisor: The Lead Mill the FTC Already Fined

HomeAdvisor isn't a separate thing to evaluate anymore — it's Angi's lead-selling arm, running the same shared-lead, billed-on-send playbook. The difference is HomeAdvisor is the one the federal government actually fined for it. Here's the receipt.

Verdict —<br>No

For most contractors, no. HomeAdvisor (now part of Angi) sells shared leads, bills you whether the homeowner ever answers, and — this is the part no marketing blog leads with — paid the FTC up to $7.2 million to settle charges it sold contractors leads it misrepresented as high-quality. When the regulator has already written the verdict, you don't need ours. Catch your own leads instead.

Finding 01

It's Angi now — same machine

HomeAdvisor and Angie's List merged into Angi. HomeAdvisor is the lead-generation side: homeowners request a service, and their contact info is sold — shared — to several contractors at once, billed on send. If you've read our Angi review, you already know the model. What makes HomeAdvisor worth its own page is the one fact that outranks every pricing table: the FTC.

The fact that ends the debate

On the record
“High-quality leads from homeowners ready to hire.”
In 2023, HomeAdvisor agreed to pay the FTC up to $7.2 million to settle charges that it used deceptive tactics to sell leads to service providers — including leads that weren't for the service the contractor offered, or weren't in their area. That's not a disgruntled Reddit post. That's a federal settlement.

Finding 03

What to do instead

You don't have to rent leads from a company the FTC fined. Catch the ones you already earn:

  1. Answer fast and turn on missed-call text-back (~$30/mo) so no lead slips to the next contractor.
  2. Ask every customer for a Google review — it ranks you in the map pack, where the leads are free.
  3. Make sure your site actually converts the traffic your truck and sign already create.
  4. If you test any lead service, cap it hard and track close rate per source — then kill what doesn't pay.
