Jobber: The Best First Software for a Small Shop

Not every tool sold to you is a scam. Jobber is the one most small shops should actually run — quote, schedule, invoice, and get paid from one app your crew can use without a training weekend. Here's the honest cost and the catch.

Verdict —<br>Buy it (small shops)

For a solo-to-mid crew, yes. Jobber is the cleanest all-in-one for quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and CRM, it's genuinely easy, and it scores high on user satisfaction. The catch: extra users run ~$29/mo each and some features are gated to pricier tiers, so price the plan for the crew you have. Start on the free trial, not the top plan.

Finding 01

What you actually get

Jobber is a field-service operating system for home-service businesses: quoting, scheduling and dispatch, invoicing, payments, and a client CRM, in one web + mobile app. It replaces the clipboard, the text-message schedule, and the “I'll invoice you later” that never happens. For a one-to-fifteen-person shop it's the most common honest answer to “what software should I use?” — and it's a real tool, not a lead mill renting you your own customers.

The honest catch

Fair warning
“One simple plan for your whole business.”
Not quite. Extra users are ~$29/mo each, and some things (like GPS tracking) sit on higher tiers even for one user. The gap between month-to-month and annual pricing is wide, so pay annual once you're sure. None of this is dishonest — it's just how the bill grows. Price the plan for the crew you actually have.

Questions, answered straight

Is Jobber worth it for a small contractor?
For most solo-to-mid crews, yes. It brings quoting, scheduling, invoicing, payments, and CRM into one easy app and scores high on user satisfaction. Start on Core or Connect and a free trial; only move up tiers when the added features clearly pay for themselves.
How much does Jobber cost in 2026?
Roughly $29–$49/mo for Core (1 user), ~$99–$139 for Connect (up to 5), ~$149–$199 for Grow (up to 10), and ~$399–$499 for Plus (up to 15), billed month-to-month; annual billing saves about 25–40%. Extra users are ~$29/mo each. Confirm current pricing on getjobber.com.
Jobber or Housecall Pro?
Jobber tends to win on price, ease of use, and customer-facing CRM and is the better starting point for small crews. Housecall Pro offers more breadth and built-in financing tools but costs more. See our side-by-side comparison.
