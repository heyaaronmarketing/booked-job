Smith.ai: The AI Receptionist With a Human Net

If you're going to run an AI receptionist at all, this is the one that fails least — because it's a hybrid: AI answers, real humans catch what the bot drops. That reliability is also what makes the bill unpredictable. Here's the honest read before you hand it your phone.

Verdict —<br>The least-bad bot

If you must run AI, run this one. Smith.ai's human-backed hybrid is the most reliable of the AI receptionists — real agents escalate when the AI's out of its depth, so you get far fewer phantom bookings. The catch is per-call billing: transfers (~$3 each), add-ons, and spam calls make the monthly bill hard to predict. Still cheaper than a bad AI losing you a $9k job — but pricier than the boring $30 fix.

Finding 01

Why the human net matters

Most AI receptionists fail the same way: the moment a call goes off-script, the bot invents an answer or confirms a booking that never lands. Smith.ai's design hedges that — it's a hybrid. The AI handles the routine; real humans take over when it gets complicated. That single feature is why it earns the highest ratings in the category (G2 ~4.8, Capterra ~4.8, Trustpilot ~4.3) and why, if you're set on AI, it's the honest pick. It's not magic — it just doesn't leave a robot alone with your money.
