Questions, answered straight

What's the best AI receptionist for contractors in 2026?
Of the AI options, Smith.ai is the most reliable because it's a hybrid — real humans escalate when the AI is out of its depth, so you get far fewer phantom bookings. Rosie is the cheapest honest entry, and Goodcall is the easiest to set up. But for most one- and two-truck shops, the best answer is still none of them — missed-call text-back plus a fast human callback is cheaper and more reliable.
Should a small contractor use an AI receptionist at all?
Usually not first. The documented failure modes (phantom bookings, hallucinated info) quietly cost small shops jobs they never hear about. Fix your own fast call response with missed-call text-back (~$30/mo) first; consider an AI (ideally a human-backed hybrid) only if you genuinely can't staff the phones and you test it hard.
Which AI receptionist is cheapest?
Rosie starts at $49/mo for answering (booking requires the $149 Scale tier). Goodcall is $79/mo priced per unique caller. Smith.ai has a free 25-call tier but bills per call after that, so the cheapest headline isn't always the cheapest in practice.
