How to read it

A "$35 lead" you win 2 times in 10 is a $175 job acquisition. The same $35 lead at a 1-in-12 close is $420. Cheap leads with low close rates (shared leads sold to a dozen pros) are usually the most expensive customers you can buy. Full breakdown: cost per lead vs cost per booked job and is Angi worth it?
