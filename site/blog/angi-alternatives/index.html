Google Local Service Ads: the strongest paid swap

Exclusive-ish calls, pay per lead, at the top of the results page.

LSA puts you above regular ads for 'near me' searches, charges per lead rather than per click, and disputes are refundable. The catch: you still rent the demand — the moment you pause, the calls stop. Use it to fill the schedule while you build what follows.

Your Google Business Profile: the free Angi

The map pack is where homeowners actually pick.

A complete profile with steady reviews is the highest-ROI marketing a contractor can do. 91% of consumers won't consider a shop under 4 stars (BrightLocal 2026), and competitive HVAC markets take ~519 reviews to top — start the review habit now, because the compounding takes months. Exclusive leads, zero per-lead cost.

Referrals + your own website: the moat

Owned channels don't disappear when you stop paying.

A systematized referral ask (at the moment the job's done) and a website that converts — number up top, proof on page one — turn every finished job into the next one. Combined with speed to lead (respond in 5 minutes: up to 100x more likely to connect — MIT Sloan 2026), this stack beats any lead platform on cost per booked job.

Frequently asked questions

What's the best alternative to Angi for contractors?

For paid leads: Google Local Service Ads (~$168 per booked job vs Angi's ~$542). For the long term: your Google Business Profile, a referral system, and a converting website — exclusive leads you own.

Is Thumbtack better than Angi?

Usually somewhat: ~$250 per booked job vs ~$542, with more control over which leads you pay for. But it's still rented demand — the same math favors owned channels over time.

Can I get contractor leads without paying per lead?

Yes: a complete Google Business Profile with steady reviews, a referral system, and a website that converts. They compound for months and the leads are exclusively yours.
