Complete every field

Google ranks complete profiles over lazy ones.

Services, service areas, hours, description, categories — fill it all. A complete profile is the baseline for showing up in the map pack, where homeowners actually pick a contractor.

Photos + weekly posts

Fresh signals = more calls.

Add job photos every week and post offers/tips/finished jobs like it's social. Fresh-photo, actively-posted profiles get more calls and direction requests — it's free real estate on the results page.

Reviews are the ranking fuel

Steady, recent, keyword-rich.

91% won't consider a shop under 4 stars (BrightLocal 2026), and recent reviews with keywords ('water heater install') push you up the pack. Ask every customer, one-tap link, never gate or pay (fake reviews are illegal as of FTC 2024).

Frequently asked questions

How do I rank my contractor business on Google Maps?

Complete every profile field, add job photos and posts weekly, and build steady recent reviews with service keywords. 91% of homeowners won't consider a shop under 4 stars (BrightLocal 2026).

How often should I post on Google Business Profile?

Weekly — offers, tips, finished jobs. Active, fresh-photo profiles earn more calls and direction requests than static ones.

Do Google Business Profile reviews affect ranking?

Yes — count, recency, and keyword relevance all influence the map pack. Steady velocity beats a big old pile that's gone quiet.
